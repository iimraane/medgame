# Dietetics Level Framework

Each level is a theme and difficulty frame, never a fixed patient or single answer. A session builder selects one private need, compatible context contributors, response style, evidence bundle and broad success signals from a curated blueprint pool. Every level has at least six strong blueprints; a local recent-history queue excludes a used blueprint until the pool is exhausted.

| Level | Theme frame | Private need directions | Difficulty and visual evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Overflowing routine | missed meal planning, commuting intake, hydration rhythm, irregular breaks, end-of-day hunger, simple preparation barrier | Directly describable everyday routine. Conversation is primary; no visual tool yet. |
| 2 | Afternoon dip | changing lunch timing, long meetings, drink habits, snack timing, concentration concerns, recovery after rushed mornings | Need is still fairly legible. The Gallery unlocks and shows exactly two recent meal photos. |
| 3 | After a false start | rules that were too rigid, family schedule mismatch, weekend disruption, boredom, preparation friction, unrealistic self-expectations | Several plausible causes. Gallery evidence may support, but not solve, the discussion. |
| 4 | Eating under pressure | screen meals, social pressure, stress rhythm, late work, sleep disruption, emotional context | Context must be explored rather than assumed. Rhythm Journal unlocks and can clarify a selected day. |
| 5 | Everyday vegetarian choices | time pressure, shared household preferences, shopping cadence, cooking confidence, meal variety, practical availability | Multiple viable practical routes. Gallery and Journal can show different angles. |
| 6 | Worrying check-up | uncertainty after a result, family history concern, appointment preparation, conflicting online advice, prevention goals, fear of doing harm | Player must avoid diagnosis and keep coordination appropriate. Shopping Receipt unlocks as contextual material. |
| 7 | Shifted rhythm | day/night rotations, recovery sleep, commute logistics, appetite variability, shared-meal disruption, planning fatigue | Evidence can appear contradictory until chronology is understood. Tools must support, not force, a routine. |
| 8 | Weight in real life | stigma from prior care, changing activity, social meals, body-image pressure, comfort eating, energy and time constraints | Signals are less explicit and language must remain non-judgmental. Pantry & Fridge unlocks to reveal availability and preparation friction. |
| 9 | When eating becomes difficult | avoidance, loss of appetite, food preoccupation, isolation, stressful transition, uncertainty about asking for help | Sensitive signs stay subtle. More than one compassionate route is valid, but a secure next-step action remains required. |
| 10 | Incomplete file | fatigue, treatments in daily life, low resources, unstable schedule, limited support, overlapping care needs | Ambiguous multi-constraint scenario. Constraints Calendar unlocks; the player must work with uncertainty and preserve safe coordination. |

## Session assembly rules

1. Select one blueprint, not independent random facts. A blueprint carries a patient identity, one private need direction, two or three compatible contributors, a response style and approved evidence.
2. Select a personality layer independently only when compatible: direct, guarded, scattered, over-prepared, ambivalent, pragmatic, self-critical, exhausted, private, sociable, anxious, or concise. It changes disclosure style, not truth.
3. Select exactly two meal assets for Gallery cases. Other tools use only content listed in the selected blueprint and asset manifest.
4. The patient prompt receives the selected truth plus tool evidence already consulted. The debrief receives the full transcript plus used-tool events, never an expected phrase.
5. A player can succeed by different paths: careful questioning, reflection, contextual exploration, collaborative options and an appropriately safe next step. No route is a mandatory scripted dialogue.

## Consultation tool progression

- **Gallery (level 2):** two static meal photos, selected for the active blueprint.
- **Rhythm Journal (level 4):** an interactive day timeline with selected time blocks, meals, travel, work and fatigue context.
- **Shopping Receipt (level 6):** a fictional receipt/pantry purchase record constructed from the variant, without brand claims or medical interpretation.
- **Pantry & Fridge (level 8):** inspectable zones showing what is available, what requires preparation and which household constraints exist.
- **Constraints Calendar (level 10):** a weekly schedule view with work, travel, care, social meals and practical constraints.

All tools are optional, used once per session, and record only server-authorized evidence. They provide a visual source to discuss, not a diagnosis, score shortcut or answer reveal.
