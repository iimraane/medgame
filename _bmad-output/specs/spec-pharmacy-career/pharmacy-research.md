# Research guardrails

## Design implications

French professional guidance frames dispensing as an individualised information-and-advice interaction. Advice can include posology, administration, and precautions, and the pharmacist must not diagnose; this supports the game’s “counter decision and counselling” loop rather than a disease-guessing loop. [Ordre national des pharmaciens — Article R.4235-7](https://www.ordre.pharmacien.fr/l-ordre/la-deontologie/le-code-de-deontologie-commente/article-r.-4235-7-devoir-d-information-et-de-conseil)

ANSM safe-use material reinforces that medicines and advice depend on context, including other medicines, allergies, pregnancy, changing health conditions, timing between doses, and duration. This supports case-specific counselling weights and orientation outcomes rather than generic advice cards. [ANSM — Bon usage des médicaments](https://ansm.sante.fr/uploads/2023/06/06/depliant-bon-usage.pdf)

## Content rules derived from research

- Do not turn client dialogue into a diagnostic interview.
- Keep product/action suitability and safety facts canonical and server-authored.
- Do not publish broad real-world dosage or contraindication claims without catalog-level source review for the exact product, population, and context.
- Prescription-only products must be handled only through a fictional valid prescription or renewal context; they cannot be converted into a product-guessing request.
- The campaign must exclude unsafe contexts rather than rewarding a refusal/orientation path, because the selected game rule does not make that action playable.
